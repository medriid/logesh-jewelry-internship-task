The mark

One shape, three readings. A lens. A ring. The letter O. It is not a logo placed beside a name — it is a letter in the name, so the mark and the wordmark can never drift apart.

What makes it a lens and not a wedding band is the second, lighter ring set inboard of the bezel: glass sits recessed in its housing. The polish comes from the bezel’s own gradient sweeping champagne to bronze around the circumference — contrast internal to the shape, so it survives on parchment and on ink alike. A pale highlight stroke was tried first and vanished on white.
